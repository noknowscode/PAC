# Design Context: PacConsole

Confirmed by the user, 2026-10-15.

- **Audience:** demo / showcase. An Uno Platform showcase of a plant control console (PAC plant 02), with simulated telemetry. Judged by visitors, reviewers and demo audiences.
- **Use cases:** see a plant at a glance, pick a unit (tag, tile or keyboard), read its live telemetry and alert state, orbit the plant model, adjust setpoints and valves.
- **Tone:** open to a new direction. The current direction (dark "hologram" console: cyan line work, amber selection, coral alerts, Chakra Petch + IBM Plex Mono) is a starting point, not a constraint.
- **Illustration rule:** every plant drawing (hero viewport, ISO / elevation / plan thumbnails, unit preview) follows the hero's style: the solid, shaded, depth-ordered Anatomy figure look (SPEC.md).
- **Quality bar for polish passes:** fix safe issues directly; structural or identity changes go to a list for the user's call.

## Direction 2: instrument, not command center (2026-10-15)

The user asked for a calmer, science- and data-led console in the spirit of Naoto Fukasawa, keeping the anatomy plant illustrations.

- **Subject and borrowed world:** a thermal plant read like a lab instrument and a journal figure plate. The rule taken from the figure plate: each drawing is a figure with a plain caption beneath it ("Fig. 1"), and readings are set like a datasheet (label above, value, unit). The rule taken from Fukasawa (±0, MUJI): objects disappear into use. Controls are matte filled surfaces with no outlines, there is one orange indicator, and nothing is decorative.
- **Removed as command-center tells:** the glowing cyan, the panel and card outlines on every block, letterspaced all-caps labels, the Chakra Petch display face, the grid backdrop, the bordered alert pill and the boxed tags.
- **Colour (light is now the default).**
  - Light: ground #F4F5F6, surface #FFFFFF, ink #1E2833, soft ink #3E4B5A, muted #5A6573, hairline #D9DDE2, control fill #E9ECEF, accent orange #C2410C, alert #B42318.
  - Graphite (dark): ground #16181B, surface #202327, ink #E8EAEC, muted #A0A7AF, accent #F0793A, alert #F26B5E.
  - Orange means "selected" or "live" only. Alert is always paired with the word "alert" or a dashed outline.
- **Type:** one family, IBM Plex Sans (Regular, Medium, SemiBold). Its default digits are tabular (every digit is 600 units wide), so live figures don't shift. The ramp is closed: Caption 12, Body 13, Title 16, Figure 22, Display 56. Labels use sentence case; unit codes stay as written (CND-05).
- **Composition: a figure plate plus a datasheet.**
  - The plant is Fig. 1, uncontained on the ground, and the anchor (over 40% of the area).
  - The readings column has no cards: sections are separated by space and single hairline rules.
  - Net output is the one display-scale figure (56 / 22 / 13).
- **Signature:** the figure callout. A hairline leader ends in a quiet label plate. The selected unit's plate turns orange, the one orange object on the page.
