# Design Context: PacConsole

Confirmed by the user, 2026-10-15.

- **Audience:** demo / showcase. An Uno Platform showcase of a plant control console (PAC plant 02), with simulated telemetry. Judged by visitors, reviewers and demo audiences.
- **Use cases:** see a plant at a glance, pick a unit (tag, tile or keyboard), read its live telemetry and alert state, orbit the plant model, adjust setpoints and valves.
- **Tone:** open to a new direction. The current direction (dark "hologram" console: cyan line work, amber selection, coral alerts, Chakra Petch + IBM Plex Mono) is a starting point, not a constraint.
- **Illustration rule:** every plant drawing (hero viewport, ISO / elevation / plan thumbnails, unit preview) follows the hero's style: the solid, shaded, depth-ordered Anatomy figure look (SPEC.md).
- **Quality bar for polish passes:** fix safe issues directly; structural or identity changes go to a list for the user's call.
